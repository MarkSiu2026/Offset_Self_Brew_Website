The process

Four Step To Your Perfect Cup

We keep the process simple. You focus on the coffee — we handle everything else.

01

Bring or buy beans

Arrive with your own single origin, or select from our rotating roster of seasonal specialty beans sourced from the best Australian roasters.

02

Barista grinds

Our barista dials in your grind on whichever burr grinder you choose. Tell us your brew method and dose — we'll set it precisely.

03

Set up & brew

Take your station. Choose your dripper, load a filter paper, set the scale, heat the kettle to your exact temperature. Then pour.

04

Drink it

Sit down and drink the best cup you've made. 15–20 minutes of focused, intentional brewing. Every time.
