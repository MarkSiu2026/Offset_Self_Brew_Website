The process

Four steps to
your perfect cup

We keep the process simple. You focus on the coffee — we handle everything else.

01

Bring or buy beans

Arrive with your own single origin, or select from our rotating roster of seasonal specialty beans sourced from the best Australian roasters.

02

Barista grinds

Our barista dials in your grind on whichever burr grinder you choose. Tell us your brew method and dose — we'll set it precisely.

03

Set up & brew

Take your station. Choose your dripper, load a filter paper, set the scale, heat the kettle to your exact temperature. Then pour.

04

Drink it

Sit down and drink the best cup you've made. 15–20 minutes of focused, intentional brewing. Every time.

Good to know

The details
that matter

Every session is designed to give you focus and control. Here's everything you need before you arrive.

Station rules
  • Sessions are 15–20 minutes per person
  • One station per party at a time
  • Barista assists with grind — not the brew
  • Clean your station after your session
  • No outside food or drinks at the brew station
  • Under-18s welcome with a guardian
Your beans
  • Any roast profile, any origin — your call
  • We grind fresh per session, never pre-ground
  • Whole beans only — no pre-ground please
  • Minimum ~18g per session recommended
  • Store-bought or specialty — both welcome
Our beans
  • Rotating from Australian specialty roasters
  • All listed with roast date and tasting notes
  • Natural, washed, and experimental options
  • Barista will walk you through each bean
  • Buy a full retail bag to take home
Equipment notes
  • All equipment cleaned between sessions
  • Do not adjust grinder settings yourself
  • Filter papers are provided and single-use
  • Tell the barista your target water temperature
  • Scales are calibrated daily
